## Background

The project addressed multi-AGV coordination in an automated warehouse, combining order assignment, collision-free routing, and transportation-cost optimization.

## Contributions

- Reviewed more than one hundred papers on multi-AGV scheduling, routing, and obstacle avoidance.
- Formulated a multi-objective optimization model for an automated warehouse.
- Used an improved genetic algorithm for AGV assignment and designed an obstacle-avoidance strategy for collision-free routing.

## Results

Twenty AGVs completed all orders without collision on a 20×32 map while reducing total travel distance.
