## Background

This undergraduate thesis studied coupon-type recommendation and customer usage-intention prediction under class imbalance in fuel-station marketing data.

## Contributions

- Improved SMOTE to address imbalanced marketing samples.
- Combined XGBoost with particle swarm optimization to build prediction and recommendation models.
- Evaluated accuracy and F1 against nine alternatives, including random forests.

## Results

The improved model outperformed the comparison methods in accuracy and F1, reaching approximately 0.76 accuracy.
