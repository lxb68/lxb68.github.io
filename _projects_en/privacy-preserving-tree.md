## Background

Under homomorphic encryption, both decision-tree thresholds and input features remain encrypted, preventing the direct comparisons used in plaintext inference.

## Contributions

- Designed a privacy-preserving decision-tree inference scheme for encrypted comparisons.
- Evaluated the approach on three datasets.
- Analyzed how runtime changed with tree depth.

## Results

Experiments confirmed that encrypted inference produced the same results as plaintext inference.
