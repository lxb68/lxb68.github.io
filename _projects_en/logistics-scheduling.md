## Background

This national industry–university research project addressed automatic cargo assignment and online scheduling while minimizing the number of vehicle trips and total travel distance.

## Contributions

- Formulated a multi-objective optimization model for total trips and distance.
- Independently built the AnyLogic model and conducted simulation experiments.
- Visualized vehicle waiting and loading-point utilization through charts and a real-time 3D model.
- Led the team during the project's second year.

## Outcomes

The work led to patent and software-copyright outputs, as well as two provincial and three university-level competition awards.
